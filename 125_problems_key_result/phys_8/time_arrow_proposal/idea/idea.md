# Idea Agent Result: Resolution-Aware Arrow-of-Time Attribution Benchmark

## Search context

The Survey Agent established that the time arrow is not a single scalar phenomenon. Its empirical manifestations combine nonequilibrium entropy production, preparation conditions, inaccessible degrees of freedom, record formation, and a cosmological low-gravitational-entropy boundary problem. The idea search was therefore constrained to directions that can test attribution claims without claiming a laboratory solution of cosmology.

## Candidate portfolio

1. **Single-estimator entropy dashboard.** Compute one trajectory asymmetry score across systems. Rejected as the primary direction because a score without an observation and completeness contract cannot distinguish physical dissipation from misspecification (G1, G2).
2. **Record-cost program.** Relate stable records to environmental decoherence and information-theoretic costs. Retained as a competitive subprogram, but it cannot independently adjudicate the cosmological boundary condition (G3, G4).
3. **Cosmological gravitational-entropy toy models.** Compare structure-growth proxies under alternative entropy proposals. Retained as high-risk context; model dependence makes it insufficient as the near-term primary program (G4).
4. **Resolution-Aware Arrow-of-Time Attribution Benchmark (RAAT).** Construct a common model card, observation ladder, and cross-family diagnostic set for equilibrium, driven stochastic, hidden-state, and open-quantum generators. Selected because it tests whether an apparent arrow survives model completion, protocol reversal, and coarsening (G1--G6).

## Selected primary direction

**Title:** Resolution-Aware Arrow-of-Time Attribution Benchmark (RAAT)

**Central hypothesis:** If a temporal asymmetry is caused by resolved nonequilibrium driving, then its support will remain coherent across valid observation resolutions, agree with an independently specified entropy-production ledger, and survive protocol-reversal and nuisance-injection tests. If it is generated primarily by incomplete observation, preparation asymmetry, or a misspecified reversal operation, at least one of those tests will fail or become non-identifiable.

**Scientific object:** Finite trajectory ensembles from declared stochastic and open-quantum generators; the cosmological arrow is represented only by a boundary-condition ledger and a toy comparator.

**Mechanism:** Compare forward and reversed path probabilities, fluctuation-relation residuals, record-persistence indicators, and resolution sensitivity under shared model cards.

**Falsifiers:**

- Competing generators remain non-identifiable under all declared observation maps.
- A nominal arrow reverses or disappears when the physical protocol is represented symmetrically.
- The conclusion changes discontinuously under reasonable hidden-state, temperature, or sampling perturbations.
- The claimed cosmological correspondence requires treating a toy boundary parameter as observed cosmological evidence.

**Expected contribution:** A negative-control-rich decision protocol that says when a finite dataset supports dissipation, only supports descriptive asymmetry, or cannot support attribution.

## Simulated deliberation record

The simulated search first favored a broad ``entropy is the arrow'' narrative. Evidence constraints rejected this as too strong. A second pass separated boundary conditions, dynamics, observations, and records. RAAT was selected because it converts that conceptual separation into discriminating tests rather than a polished restatement of the Second Law. The final portfolio preserves the record-cost and gravitational-toy directions as competitive/high-risk components rather than silently discarding them.
