# Idea Agent: Resolution-Ladder Model Comparison

## Candidate portfolio

| Candidate | Scientific operation | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Single-channel high-mass Drell--Yan contact-interaction fit | Uses a precise but narrow high-energy tail | Retain as an analysis module; alone it cannot diagnose cross-channel or EFT-validity ambiguities. |
| Excited-lepton benchmark scan | Separates a resonant/excited-state target from contact interactions | Retain as a discriminating module, not as a universal compositeness test. |
| Lepton-hadron resolution projection | Uses deep-inelastic and partonic-structure observables | Retain for complementary nuisance control; future capability cannot be treated as observed evidence. |
| **Resolution-Ladder Model Comparison (RLMC)** | Fits pointlike-SM, EFT contact, form-factor, and excited-fermion hypotheses on a shared resolution and nuisance ledger | **Selected primary direction.** |

## Selected direction

RLMC is a computational inference design that converts the question "are quarks and leptons fundamental?" into a hierarchy of falsifiable likelihood comparisons. It matches channels by momentum transfer and observable definition, enforces an EFT-validity gate, and treats PDFs, detector response, luminosity, theory scale choices, and background normalizations as explicit nuisance objects. It compares four hypothesis families: pointlike SM; contact operators below a stated cutoff; finite form factors; and excited-fermion templates.

The central hypothesis is that a shared resolution ledger and cross-channel likelihood will discriminate genuine, coherent substructure-like patterns from artifacts of EFT truncation or channel-specific systematics more reliably than independent single-channel limits. The pointlike-SM hypothesis remains falsifiable: a reproducible cross-channel departure that survives the validity and nuisance stress tests would require model revision. Conversely, a null or non-identifiable comparison is a range-specific constraint, not a declaration that a final constituent has been found.

## Falsifiers and guardrails

RLMC fails if the competing hypotheses are non-identifiable in the declared kinematic regime, if cutoff variation or dimension-eight terms reverse the inference, if nuisance perturbations reproduce the apparent signal, or if channels require incompatible coefficient patterns without a stated UV model. It rejects any candidate that treats a Wilson coefficient as proof of a preon, claims future collider projections are data, or describes a null result as proof of absolute indivisibility.
