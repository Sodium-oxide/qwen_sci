# ExperimentDesign Agent: RLMC Research Design

## Boundary and research brief

RLMC is a computational_digital research design. It specifies a reproducible likelihood, synthetic closure tests, and a future public-data-compatible analysis interface. It does not operate an accelerator, control a beam or detector, retrieve restricted event data, or report a new exclusion or discovery.

## Resolution-ladder inputs

Each channel is represented by a declared momentum-transfer variable, binning, fiducial selection, response model, and nuisance ledger. The baseline is pointlike SM prediction. Alternatives are: dimension-six and declared dimension-eight contact operators; a low-momentum-transfer form-factor expansion; and a benchmark excited-fermion template. Primary observables are differential rate and angular-shape summaries in neutral and charged Drell--Yan-like channels, with optional lepton-hadron or future-facility projections explicitly marked as projections.

## Inference, validation, and decision rule

The workflow first generates synthetic records from each hypothesis and nuisance setting. It tests whether the model families can be distinguished before fitting any approved data interface. It then compares models with held-out kinematic bins, cutoff sweeps, operator-order ablations, and adversarial PDF/detector perturbations. A claim of a preferred non-SM mechanism is permitted only when the deviation is identifiable, cutoff stable, nuisance robust, and cross-channel coherent under a stated model. Otherwise the permitted conclusion is a range- and model-dependent constraint or an indeterminate comparison.

## Human review and safety

All physical execution is prohibited. Any later use of public data must be checked by a qualified collider phenomenologist and experimental analyst for data-release terms, fiducial definitions, likelihood validity, detector response, and statistical coverage. Any accelerator or detector operation remains outside this system.
