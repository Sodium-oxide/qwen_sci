# Q1 Mathematical Model: Reduced Coherence-Weighted Pairing Kernel

The v0 model evaluates `S(p)=exp(-((p-p_AF)/sigma_AF)^2)` and
`Z_AN(p)=1-A_PG exp(-((p-p_PG)/sigma_PG)^2)`. The baseline is
`lambda_H0=lambda0*S`; the proposed relation is `lambda_H1=lambda0*S*Z_AN`.
Both use `Tc_model=Omega*exp(-1/lambda)`. The model is a deterministic
scenario comparator. Its constants are declared model assumptions and are not
material fits, literature parameters, or experimental observations.
