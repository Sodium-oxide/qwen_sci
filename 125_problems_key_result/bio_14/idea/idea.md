# Idea: ORIGIN - Organismal Reconstruction of Integrated Generative Neuroaffect

## Research problem

The phrase "where emotions originate" encourages a false localization problem. A better scientific target is to identify the generative architecture by which an organism transforms cues and bodily state into coordinated physiology, action, learning, and report. Existing studies often measure only one part of this loop, use a single emotion label as ground truth, or compare only one theoretical family. The proposed program asks whether a joint brain-body model can predict and explain emotion episodes more accurately than localized, category-only, or generic-arousal alternatives.

## Central hypothesis

**H1:** Affective episodes are better characterized as context-sensitive, distributed latent states than as activation of a single dedicated brain region or a single autonomic signature.

**H2:** A model that includes interoceptive prediction, allostatic demand, memory/context, and action policy will predict held-out multichannel observations and emotion reports better than a model driven only by stimulus category or global arousal. The corresponding experiment must manipulate both expected and measured bodily input; external cue reversal alone is not sufficient for this test.

**H3:** Evolved functional systems remain biologically important constraints. Threat, reward, and loss/evaluation tasks will not be interchangeable; however, their signatures will be distributed, partially overlapping, and modified by context and learned priors.

## ORIGIN framework

| Letter | Principle | Operational consequence |
|---|---|---|
| O | Observable operationalization | Predefine separate outcomes for neural patterns, physiology, behavior, and reported category/intensity. |
| R | Regulation and allostasis | Model energy and autonomic regulation as part of the state, not as nuisance only. |
| I | Interoception and neuromodulation | Record cardiac, electrodermal, respiratory, pupillary, and endocrine context with compatible temporal models. |
| G | Generative prediction and learning | Manipulate contextual priors and uncertainty; compare predictive-state models with sensory-only models. |
| I | Integrated distributed circuits | Estimate multiregion and multichannel patterns rather than search for one "emotion area." |
| N | Null and competing models | Require preregistered comparison with generalized arousal, attention, motor, and demand-characteristic models. |

## Novel contribution

The innovation is methodological rather than a claim of a newly discovered emotion mechanism. A single preregistered protocol will place three model families under the same evidential standard:

1. A **functional-circuit model** with condition-specific pathway features.
2. A **distributed representational model** with multivariate neural and physiological state patterns.
3. A **predictive-interoceptive model** in which context and expected bodily state influence the latent affective state.
4. A **general arousal/task-demand null** that must be beaten before claiming emotion-specific explanatory value.

Each family produces numerical out-of-sample predictions, calibration estimates, reliability estimates, and falsifiable failure conditions. This moves the debate from verbal categories toward model comparison.

## What counts as support or falsification

Support for ORIGIN requires all of the following:

* Improvement in preregistered held-out predictive performance over the general-arousal null.
* Replicable multimodal patterns across participants and a held-out context subset.
* Evidence that contextual priors and a mild, screened respiratory interoceptive perturbation explain variance beyond sensory intensity, motor preparation, attention, and measured respiratory mechanics.
* No conclusion that a single measure uniquely identifies an emotion category.

The framework is weakened if condition labels are decodable only in the training set, if performance vanishes after matching effort and motor action, if physiological measures merely track non-affective task demands, or if a simpler stimulus/arousal model has equal predictive performance. A null result is informative: it would challenge the claim that the chosen measures or task operationalize a stable integrated affective state.

## Ethical and translational rationale

The first study uses low-risk laboratory stimuli and excludes attempts to induce severe distress. Its purpose is measurement science, not diagnosis. If a robust multichannel model is established, later work could investigate transdiagnostic dysregulation of brain-body control in anxiety, depression, trauma-related syndromes, chronic pain, or autonomic disorders. Such translation requires independent clinical validation and must not convert a research score into an individual diagnostic or treatment decision.

## Design-only boundary

This is a proposal. No participant has been enrolled; no effect size, decoding accuracy, biomarker, or intervention benefit is claimed. The final report preserves this boundary and describes anticipated discriminative outcomes only as preregistered decision rules.
