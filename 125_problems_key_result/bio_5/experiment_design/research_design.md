# ExperimentDesign - PAN-CURE Protocol

**DESIGN_ONLY.** No cells, animals, patient samples, intervention, clinical trial, safety finding, response, survival result, or cure has been produced by this project.

## Scenario ladder

| Scenario | Purpose | Permissible conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| S0 - target and normal-tissue atlas | Define target/recognition distribution, tumor subtypes, and normal-tissue boundary | Measurement evidence for stated samples |
| S1 - immune mechanism core | Test recognition, killing mechanism, and dependence on target presentation | Bounded in-vitro mechanism result |
| S2 - heterogeneous model panel | Test tumor subtype, antigen-density, microenvironment, and resistance variation | Model-specific coverage and escape map |
| S3 - translational safety qualification | Evaluate product identity, persistence, cytokine/off-tumor risks, biodistribution, and toxicology in appropriate systems | Preclinical package under stated model limits |
| S4 - staged clinical study | Assess protocol-defined safety, activity, durability, and relapse with oversight | Population- and indication-specific clinical evidence |

## Hard gates

1. Target/normal-tissue measurement and provenance.
2. Mechanism confirmation and target dependence.
3. Tumor heterogeneity, escape, and microenvironment stress testing.
4. Noncompensatory normal-tissue and systemic safety evaluation.
5. Durable clinical outcome and independent confirmation.

## Controls and reporting

Use matched target-negative controls, target restoration/ablation where appropriate, irrelevant-receptor controls, normal-tissue comparators, multiple donors/batches, blinded analyses, predefined resistance challenges, and full reporting of null and toxic effects. Predefine clinical endpoints, censoring, toxicity adjudication, molecular escape analysis, and follow-up. A broad claim is blocked by missing target coverage, credible normal-tissue injury, resistant outgrowth, or absent durability.
