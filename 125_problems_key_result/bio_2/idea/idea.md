# Idea - STABLE-AGE

## Proposal

This project introduces the **Systematic Trial Architecture for Biological Longevity and Aging Evaluation (STABLE-AGE)**. It is an auditable, design-only framework for testing candidate geroscience interventions without equating a biomarker change with stopped aging. STABLE-AGE treats biological aging as a latent, multidomain trajectory observed through predeclared molecular, physiologic, functional, and clinical measures. It requires every claim to state the eligible population, intervention, comparator, horizon, assay set, missing-data policy, safety boundary, and external-support assumptions.

## Main question and hypotheses

For a defined adult population, does candidate intervention class $a$ change the rate of a preregistered multidomain aging trajectory relative to a matched control over the declared horizon, and do functional or clinical measures move in a direction that is compatible with that change without unacceptable harm?

- **H0:** observed differences are explained by baseline imbalance, measurement drift, behavior change, selective retention, regression to the mean, survival selection, or chance.
- **H1:** the intervention shifts a prespecified multidomain trajectory and shows concordant target engagement and functional evidence within its stated population and horizon.
- **H2:** a biomarker shift occurs without functional/clinical concordance, so it supports target engagement or hypothesis generation only.
- **H3:** effects are heterogeneous, context-dependent, or offset by adverse outcomes; broad anti-aging claims are therefore unsupported.

## Design contribution

The innovation is not a new purported anti-aging therapy. It is a decision architecture that prevents four recurrent inferential errors: treating chronology as biology, treating exceptional-age demography as mechanism, treating a single clock as a validated surrogate, and trading a safety breach against an average biomarker benefit. STABLE-AGE combines a preregistered endpoint hierarchy, assay calibration, a randomized comparative core where ethically feasible, long-term event follow-up, blinded adjudication, and replication in an independent cohort.

## Bounded conclusion language

Passing STABLE-AGE can support statements such as: ``In the stated population and time window, the tested intervention changed a prespecified trajectory and met the stated safety and concordance criteria.'' It cannot support statements such as: ``Aging has been stopped,'' ``humans no longer age after a particular birthday,'' or ``the intervention guarantees longevity.''
