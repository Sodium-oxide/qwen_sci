# Idea Agent - SPECTRA-Loop Portfolio

## Evidence-constrained problem statement

The useful research problem is not to defeat a universal efficiency law. It is to determine whether an architecture can reduce named loss channels while closing a common source-to-sink energy boundary. The Survey Agent supplies six accepted gaps, with the strongest cluster linking conditional-limit clarity, spectral/thermal coupling, and metrology.

## Candidate portfolio

## Selected idea

**SPECTRA-Loop** expands to **Spectral Partitioning, Energy Closure, Thermal Recovery, and Assurance**. Its research unit is:

`architecture x source-spectrum card x optical-partition card x thermal-boundary card x electrical-load card x calibration/measurement card x stability/recovery card`.

The core intervention is a predeclared optical partition that sends one spectral interval to a PV absorber and sends a defined remainder to a thermal receiver/emitter and TPV cell. The system ledger preserves reflected, transmitted, absorbed, emitted, rejected, and parasitic energy rather than treating any of them as disappearing losses. This makes a strong and useful claim possible: an apparent gain is scientifically interpretable only if it survives the same declared input boundary as its reference.

## Hypothesis and falsifiers

**H-SPECTRA-001.** Explicit closure of spectral, thermal, electrical, and metrology boundaries will make a hybrid PV-TPV efficiency claim more interpretable and comparable than a device-only electrical efficiency claim; a net improvement may be asserted only when the proposed architecture exceeds its declared reference within joint uncertainty and stability conditions.

The idea is falsified or downgraded when any of the following occurs: the energy residual cannot be reconciled within the uncertainty budget; the claimed advantage vanishes when optical and auxiliary losses are included; the TPV contribution is obtained by an undeclared additional source input; the result changes under a calibration cross-check; the operating point drifts outside the declared stability window; or the source/thermal condition cannot support a transfer claim.

## Simulated idea evolution

1. **Root:** improve a PV device's electrical efficiency.
2. **Mechanism substitution:** replace a device-only target with spectral routing plus thermal recovery, because a single absorber leaves spectral losses unresolved.
3. **Constraint repair:** add an explicit system input boundary after identifying that direct-PV and thermal-radiation denominators cannot be mixed.
4. **Assurance repair:** add calibration, uncertainty, stability, and transfer cards so that an attractive peak metric cannot become a deployment claim by wording alone.

The primary direction is selected because it is evidence-aligned, falsifiable, and designable without pretending that a physical experiment has already occurred.
