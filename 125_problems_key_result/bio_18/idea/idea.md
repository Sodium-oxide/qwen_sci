# Idea stage: evidence-constrained candidate selection

## Candidate portfolio

### IDEA-FP-01: Secular Phenotype Trend Catalogue

This route would assemble plots of cohort differences in teeth, faces, body
size, and other visible traits. It is descriptive and potentially useful for
data inventory, but it cannot distinguish inherited change from developmental
conditions, treatment, migration, or altered measurement. It does not close
GAP-FP-01 and is not selected as the primary direction.

### IDEA-FP-02: Phenotype Pathway Attribution Atlas

This selected direction proposes a preregistered, longitudinal, population-
level framework. It treats observed phenotype differences as an attribution
problem with five separate candidate pathways: demographic composition,
developmental exposure, technology or treatment, measurement process, and
optional inherited features. It evaluates whether a pathway-aware model
improves temporal and external-site prediction and calibration relative to an
age, birth-cohort, and site baseline. A dental sub-study keeps third-molar
agenesis distinct from eruption, impaction, extraction, and orthodontic
treatment.

The main claim is deliberately narrow: pathway-aware modelling may produce a
more transportable description of selected quantitative phenotypes than a
cohort-only model. The claim is falsifiable if held-out calibration does not
improve, if improvements vanish after measurement harmonisation or structure
controls, or if subgroup reliability gates fail.

### IDEA-FP-03: Universal Future Human Appearance Generator

This route would generate visual depictions of a future human. It is rejected.
There is no evidence registry that supports a universal target appearance, and
the direction would invite biological determinism, stereotype reinforcement,
and untestable claims.

## Selection rationale

IDEA-FP-02 is the only candidate that directly links all accepted Survey gaps
to observable variables and a falsification path. It honors the strongest
negative evidence in the Survey: apparent cohort differences are not enough
to establish adaptive genetic evolution, and polygenic population contrasts
are vulnerable to confounding. It is not a clinical decision system and
cannot be released for individual prediction.

No MCTS, autonomous evolutionary search, or simulated debate trace was run.
The selection record is a transparent candidate comparison so that provenance
is not fabricated.
