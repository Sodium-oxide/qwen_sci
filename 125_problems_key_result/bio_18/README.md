# Future Human Physical Phenotypes: Research Proposal Package

This package implements the repository's four-stage research workflow:
Survey, Idea, ExperimentDesign, and Author. It addresses the question
"Will humans look physically different in the future?" as a scientific,
population-level proposal rather than a deterministic forecast.

The final report is design-only. It does not contain participant data,
model fits, causal estimates, or observed experimental results.

## Scientific scope

The study distinguishes four pathways that can produce apparent changes
in human physical phenotype:

1. transgenerational allele-frequency change;
2. developmental plasticity and environmental exposure;
3. technology-mediated phenotype modification; and
4. demographic composition and measurement-process change.

The proposal focuses on pre-specified, ethically governed quantitative
phenotypes. It explicitly excludes individual appearance prediction,
ranking of population groups, and claims that a particular future human
appearance is inevitable.

## Directory map

- survey: verified evidence, claim traceability, and frozen gaps.
- idea: candidate directions, selection audit, and primary idea.
- experiment_design: preregisterable observational design and author handoff.
- author: IEEE LaTeX source, compiled PDF, rendering, and PDF validation.

## Status

All four stages are completed as a research-design record. The PDF is
the Author-stage synthesis of frozen Survey evidence and the upstream
Idea and ExperimentDesign artifacts.
