## Title

*Can More Pigments Be Discovered? An Evidence-Bound, Multiobjective Proposal for Extending the Usable Material Colour Frontier*

## Authorial contract

This Author artifact binds only to the Survey evidence plan, the Idea portfolio, and the design-only ExperimentDesign handoff. It therefore makes four controlled claims:

1. A pigment is a material and a colour is an observer-and-condition-dependent perceptual measurement; the two cannot be treated as synonyms.
2. The useful material-pigment frontier can remain open even when normal human trichromatic colour matching does not gain a new dimension.
3. The modern YInMn Blue case motivates a systematic search but does not prove that any candidate in this proposal is new.
4. A claim of practical novelty should require reference-library comparison, stated CIE conditions, raw spectra, engineering/constraint gates, uncertainty, and authorised measurement.

The report proposes a calibrated Pareto search and a future validation study. It contains no executed experiment, simulation, material synthesis, physical characterisation result, safety approval, or observed performance value.
