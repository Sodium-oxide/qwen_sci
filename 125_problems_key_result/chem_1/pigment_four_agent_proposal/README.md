# Four-Agent Research Proposal: Discovering New Pigments Without Claiming New Human Colors

**Research topic.** *Are more colour pigments to discover?* The proposal distinguishes a new chemical pigment, a new reflectance spectrum, and a newly discriminable human colour appearance. It uses YInMn Blue as a motivating case, not as proof that the human visual system has acquired a new primary colour.

## Scope and boundary

The artifacts are a simulated four-agent run based on cited literature and public technical standards. They are not experimental results, a laboratory operating procedure, safety authorization, or a claim that a new pigment has been made. All numerical thresholds in the design are preregistered targets or decision rules, not observed values.

## Provenance

- **Topic supplied by user:** new pigments versus new colours; Prussian Blue history; 2009 YInMn Blue discovery.
- **Primary colour-science standard checked:** CIE 015:2018, *Colorimetry, 4th Edition*, DOI `10.25039/TR.015.2018`.
- **YInMn record checked:** M. A. Subramanian and C. C. Li, *YInMn blue—200 Years in the making: New intense inorganic pigments based on chromophores in trigonal bipyramidal coordination*, *Materials Today Advances*, vol. 16, 100323, 2022, DOI `10.1016/j.mtadv.2022.100323`.
- Direct publisher pages for the YInMn paper were protected by CAPTCHA during this run. The record is therefore used as bibliographic and conceptual evidence, while any unverified property values are deliberately excluded.
