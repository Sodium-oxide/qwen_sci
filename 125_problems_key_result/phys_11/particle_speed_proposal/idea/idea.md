# Idea Agent Output: Energy-to-Velocity Saturation Ledger (EVSL)

## Candidate generation and selection

The Survey handoff prohibits searching for a lower speed cap below `c`, because the relevant relativistic relation already establishes an asymptote for massive particles. The Idea Agent therefore explored four evidence-bound directions:

1. **Architecture-specific speed records.** Rejected because the resulting comparison privileges an already saturated variable and mixes facility operating conditions.
2. **Universal accelerator-limit formula.** Rejected because ring rigidity, lepton radiation, linear gradient, plasma staging, and beam protection cannot be reduced to one source-supported scalar without hiding assumptions.
3. **Energy-to-Velocity Saturation Ledger (EVSL).** Selected. It retains exact kinematics while classifying the active engineering limitation with model cards and sensitivity declarations.
4. **Strong-field route to a different speed regime.** Retained as a high-risk boundary-analysis component, but not as a superluminal proposal; radiation reaction and QED effects must be explicit.

## Selected primary direction

**Title:** Energy-to-Velocity Saturation Ledger for Constraint-Aware Accelerator Comparison

**Target gaps:** `GAP-EVSL-001`, `GAP-CROSSPLATFORM-002`.

**Central hypothesis.** Beyond a predeclared ultra-relativistic saturation threshold, particle speed no longer differentiates credible accelerator choices. A common ledger containing Lorentz factor, `1-beta`, rigidity, energy gain and loss, power, beam quality, and protection variables can identify the dominant practical constraint of each architecture more transparently than a near-light-speed narrative.

**Mechanism.** The exact mapping from energy to beta compresses at high gamma. A machine can add large energy while changing beta imperceptibly, so the decision-relevant variables move into a separate constraint ledger. Ring cards expose `B rho` and radiative loss conditions; linear and plasma cards expose gradient, length, driver/power, staging, and energy-spread conditions; strong-field cards expose radiation-reaction applicability.

**Falsifiable predictions.** The proposal is falsified if (a) a speed-only metric remains materially discriminating after the stated saturation condition while energy/rigidity metrics do not; (b) constraint labels change across reasonable inputs without a declared sensitivity region; (c) a concept violates the exact relativistic mapping or omits an energy-loss ledger; or (d) projected performance is silently represented as measured performance.
