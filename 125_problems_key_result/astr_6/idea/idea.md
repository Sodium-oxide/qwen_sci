# Idea Agent: PERMANENT-EXTRATERRESTRIAL-HABITATION-READINESS-BENCHMARK

## Central idea

The PERMANENT-EXTRATERRESTRIAL-HABITATION-READINESS-BENCHMARK (PEHRB) evaluates a
settlement as a fault-tolerant, resource-constrained, maintainable system of systems. It
does not ask whether an inspiring destination exists. It asks whether a declared habitat,
crew, resource inventory, power architecture, surface site, and repair supply chain can
remain inside safety and inventory constraints throughout a preregistered mission horizon
after routine resupply and real-time Earth intervention are removed.

## Scenario classes

* **S0:** Earth-resupplied outpost baseline with present-style logistics and ground
  support.
* **S1:** Closed-loop ground demonstrator with no local planetary ISRU; isolates life
  support, food, maintenance, and fault-recovery closure.
* **S2:** Lunar surface scenario with site-specific power, thermal, resource, and
  radiation assumptions.
* **S3:** Martian surface scenario with atmospheric/ice/regolith inputs, seasonal power,
  ISRU, dust, and communication-delay assumptions.
* **S4:** S2/S3 plus qualified local manufacturing/repair modules and a bounded
  resupply policy.
* **S5:** Correlated-disturbance ensemble over power outages, crop loss, leaks, hardware
  failure, crew health/medical demand, resource uncertainty, and delayed repair.

## Falsifiable hypotheses

* H0: A declared outpost requires routine Earth consumables, replacement hardware, or
  continuous ground intervention to remain within critical constraints.
* H1: An integrated architecture maintains all declared resource stocks and health/
  performance thresholds over its horizon without routine consumable resupply.
* H2: An architecture that succeeds under isolated subsystem faults fails under realistic
  correlated disturbances, revealing hidden coupling.
* H3: Verified ISRU and repair capability reduce the resupply mass and extend autonomy
  without introducing larger energy, reliability, or quality risks.
* H4: A site/power combination preserves critical loads through its declared worst-case
  outage and degradation scenario.
* H5: A claimed ``permanent'' capability becomes unsupported when its system boundary
  includes unaccounted resupply, discard streams, external medical evacuation, or
  undisclosed ground intervention.

## Contribution and boundary

The benchmark output is a settlement-readiness profile: resource closure by commodity,
autonomy horizon, critical-load survival margin, repair/manufacturing coverage, health
and radiation margin, fault recovery, external logistics mass, and conditional failure
distribution. This project is DESIGN_ONLY. It does not certify a site, simulate an actual
mission, demonstrate a human health outcome, or establish that any population can now
live permanently on another planet.
