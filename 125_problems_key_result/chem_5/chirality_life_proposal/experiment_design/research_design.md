# ExperimentDesign Agent: Design-Only Protocol for Stereochemical Closure

## Scope and safety gate

This design is an evidence synthesis and conceptual-model protocol. It does **not** prescribe or run synthesis, replication assays, cell culture, directed evolution, or organism engineering. No biological material, sequence, recipe, temperature, concentration, or operational apparatus is specified. Any future physical work is outside scope and requires qualified supervision, institution-specific review, and appropriate biosafety and chemical-safety authorization.

## Research brief

- **Selected idea:** D2, operational stereochemical closure.
- **Object:** published records of chiral template copying, functional polymer behavior, enantioenrichment mechanisms, and life-definition arguments.
- **Question:** under explicitly stated chemical-life criteria, does a compatible stereochemical relation explain a necessary capability more precisely than ``one preferred hand''?
- **Execution policy:** `DESIGN_ONLY`.

## Comparator framework

Each eligible source is assigned a non-operational architecture state:

1. `homochiral_closure`: compatible same-convention polymer/catalyst/template relation.
2. `cross_chiral_closure`: reproducible complementary relation across opposite conventions.
3. `mixed_unclosed`: mixed input or interaction without supported closure.
4. `achiral_or_unknown`: explicitly achiral proposal, or evidence insufficient to assign a stereochemical architecture.

The coded capability endpoints are `template_continuity`, `reproducible_structure`, `selective_recognition`, `heritable_information`, and `open_ended_evolution_support`. An endpoint can be recorded as source-demonstrated, source-argued, not assessed, or out of scope. No endpoint is inferred from a paper title or from general knowledge.

## Primary hypothesis and decision rule

For a source-defined chiral polymer architecture, closure should be associated with fewer documented incompatibility failures and a stronger capability package than `mixed_unclosed`. The study does not test whether life in the abstract exists. Its output is a conditional assessment:

- `closure_functionally_supported`;
- `alternative_architecture_supported`;
- `evidence_insufficient`;
- `definition_sensitive_abstain`.

The prohibited output is `life_created`, `universal_law_proved`, or a statement that a mirror organism exists.

## Corpus, coding, and validation

The corpus is frozen with source type, DOI/URL, publication date, evidence passage, architecture state, capability endpoint, source limitation, and claim-language class. Two qualified reviewers independently code a pilot set; disagreements must remain visible and are resolved by documented adjudication or retained for sensitivity analysis. Reviews frame mechanisms but cannot alone populate direct experimental endpoints. A second analyst checks that later review language has not been used as an earlier source observation.

The primary analysis is a structured evidence matrix, not a pooled causal effect. It reports the number of eligible records, source type, independent evidence origins, endpoint coverage, exclusions, ambiguity, and negative cases. Sensitivity analyses remove model-only records, then remove review-only records, and then vary the operational definition of life. A conclusion survives only if the wording remains appropriate across these declared variants.

## Negative-result and stopping rules

The result is negative or indeterminate if closure has no consistent source-supported relationship with the registered endpoints; if cross-chiral cases cannot be classified faithfully; if evidence is too sparse to link polymer function to heredity; or if definition variation changes the conclusion. The correct response is to publish the limitation and preserve the evidence ledger, not to force a universal answer.
