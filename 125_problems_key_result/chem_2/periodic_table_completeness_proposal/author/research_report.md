## Title

*Will the Periodic Table Ever Be Complete? An Evidence-Aware Proposal for Mapping Conditional New-Element Reachability Beyond 118*

## Authorial contract

This report is bound to the Survey, Idea, and ExperimentDesign artifacts in this folder. It concludes that no accepted evidence fixes a final atomic number and rejects the claim that human inventiveness is the only obstacle. The report instead distinguishes physical nuclear stability, production/detection reachability, proof sufficiency, and formal institutional recognition.

It proposes a time-split calibration study of an uncertainty-aware three-layer frontier model. It contains no accelerator operation, nuclear reaction prescription, detector configuration, simulation result, physical observation, discovery priority decision, or element-naming claim. IUPAC/IUPAP remain the appropriate bodies for formal discovery assessment.

## Contents

- IUPAC's 2016 recognition and naming context for elements 113, 115, 117, and 118.
- The 2018 IUPAC/IUPAP criterion review and its implications for technology-aware proof standards.
- A four-layer definition of periodic-table completeness.
- A selected Idea portfolio and a three-layer conditional-reachability hypothesis.
- A design-only corpus, modelling, historical validation, abstention, governance, and reporting protocol.
- A minimum evidence record for any future claim.
