# Idea Agent Result: PRESSURE-ATLAS

The selected direction is PRESSURE-ATLAS: a pressure-resolved, compartment-aware atlas of deep-biosphere volume, composition, and carbon significance.

The core hypothesis is not that the deep biosphere has one global activity rate. It is that a hierarchical integration of physical habitat volume, absolute cell abundance, pressure-preserved community measurements, and pressure-corrected metabolic rates can reduce the dominant uncertainty in global significance estimates.

The portfolio comparison selected this direction over a gene-only census and a cell-count-only inventory. A gene-only census cannot distinguish potential from flux. A cell-count inventory cannot infer active carbon transformations. PRESSURE-ATLAS links the two through stratified sampling and an explicit measurement model:

    habitat volume -> absolute cells -> viable and active fractions -> process rates -> carbon and electron flux.

Every arrow receives a calibration and uncertainty requirement. The primary falsification condition is clear: if pressure-preserved and decompressed measurements cannot be reconciled by a compartment-specific correction model, a single global pressure correction is rejected and the atlas remains stratified rather than forcibly aggregated.

The selected primary idea is idea-pressure-atlas-001. Competitive ideas are an all-ocean sediment diversity map and an in situ enzyme-rate observatory. Both are useful components, but neither independently answers volume, composition, and significance.
