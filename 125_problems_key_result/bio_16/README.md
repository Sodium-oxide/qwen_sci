# bio_16: Why Do Humans Stop Growing Taller?

This package is a four-stage, evidence-bound research proposal. It does **not** report an executed pediatric study, a personal growth prediction, a causal hormone estimate, or clinical guidance.

| Stage | Main artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Survey | `survey/survey.md` | Scientific reframing, verified evidence, source boundaries, and accepted gaps. |
| Idea | `idea/idea.md` | Route comparison and selection of the Growth-Plate Closure Readiness Atlas. |
| ExperimentDesign | `experiment_design/research_design.md` | Preregistered noninterventional cohort, endpoints, validation, ethics, and stopping conditions. |
| Author | `growth_plate_closure_readiness_proposal.pdf` | Seven-page English IEEE-style proposal compiled from the supplied IEEE conference template. |

The scientific question is whether a longitudinal combination of standardized height velocity, radiation-free growth-plate MRI, puberty-related measurements, and separately consented inherited summaries improves calibrated prediction of sustained low longitudinal growth beyond chronological age and bone age. It does not claim that genes alone determine when a person stops growing.

`survey/literature_search.json` records the attempted OpenAlex and AnySearch retrieval. Both services were unavailable in this environment; no unavailable-search candidate was cited. All seven registry records were verified on PubMed before use. `author/pdf_validation.json` and `integrity_hashes.json` record final artifact checks.
