# Idea: Growth-Plate Closure Readiness Atlas

## Portfolio and selection

**IDEA-GP-01: Cross-sectional skeletal-maturity catalogue.** This route would collate chronological age, bone age, and a single imaging examination. It is inexpensive and communicable, but it cannot determine whether a marker predicts future residual growth; it only describes an existing maturity association. It therefore fails GAP-GP-01.

**IDEA-GP-02: Longitudinal Growth-Plate Closure Readiness Atlas (selected).** This proposal follows participants through repeated standardized height measurements, pubertal endocrine panels, radiation-free MRI growth-plate features, and optional inherited feature summaries. A preregistered model estimates the probability of sustained low growth velocity and interval-censored structural closure. It directly targets both accepted gaps because it is longitudinal, compares against an age-and-bone-age baseline, and requires subgroup calibration before interpretation.

**IDEA-GP-03: Direct hormone perturbation with tissue sampling.** A manipulation study might be mechanistically informative, but elective endocrine exposure or growth-plate biopsy is not an acceptable first-line research design in healthy minors. It is rejected on risk, feasibility, and inference grounds.

No Monte Carlo tree search, evolutionary search, or autonomous idea evolution was executed. The portfolio is an evidence-bound route comparison, not a fabricated optimization trajectory.

## Selected hypothesis and decision rule

The central hypothesis is predictive: after leakage controls and external validation, a preregistered multimodal model containing growth-plate imaging and puberty-related features will improve calibration and proper scoring of residual linear-growth cessation relative to a chronological-age-plus-bone-age baseline. A feature group is retained only if the improvement is stable across registered temporal and site holdouts and does not create unacceptable subgroup calibration gaps.

This is not a claim that estrogen, a genetic score, or an MRI texture causes closure. It is also not a proposal to determine an individual child's adult height or to make treatment decisions. The model is a research instrument whose outputs remain conditional on data quality, consent, imaging quality, and a human specialist review.
