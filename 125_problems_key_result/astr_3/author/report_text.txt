BENCHMARK supplies a concrete next step. It compares
only parameterized scenarios with explicit backgrounds,
initial states, parameter ledgers, and forward predictions.
Its held-out, prior-sensitive, and systematics-aware protocol
can establish comparative predictive performance, expose
underdetermined proposals, and delimit the reach of an
extrapolation. It cannot observe a first instant, identify
“before time,” or convert a past-boundary theorem into
proof of a unique cosmic origin. This paper is therefore a
reproducible research design, not an empirical model-selection
result.
