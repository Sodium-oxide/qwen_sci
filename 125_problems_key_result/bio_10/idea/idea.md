# Idea: EVOTRACE

## Central idea

**EVOTRACE** stands for **Evolutionary Trait Reconstruction and Causal Evidence**. It is a multi-scale framework for explaining evolutionary change without collapsing distinct inferences. It requires a researcher to establish, in order: a lineage and environmental scope; heritable variation; a population-frequency trajectory; ecological and reproductive consequences; a genotype-to-trait/developmental mechanism; and independent causal or predictive evaluation.

EVOTRACE is designed to answer two linked questions. The first is descriptive: which variants, traits, and lineages changed over time? The second is causal: what evidence supports selection, drift, gene flow, and developmental mechanism as contributors to that change? A complex organ is explained only when the evidence path reaches both population transmission and organismal mechanism.

## Research objective

Develop a preregistered, reproducible protocol that estimates the relative support for evolutionary forces and tests whether candidate genetic or regulatory changes mediate adaptive trait evolution in a focal lineage or a replicated set of lineages.

## Hypotheses

* **H0 - neutral/structure hypothesis:** observed allele-frequency and trait changes are compatible with drift, demographic history, and migration after accounting for sampling uncertainty.
* **H1 - selection hypothesis:** genotype and trait trajectories are more consistent with environment-dependent differential fitness than with the H0 process.
* **H2 - mediation hypothesis:** a candidate genomic change affects performance through a measurable molecular, developmental, or physiological intermediate that predicts the trait.
* **H3 - contingency hypothesis:** prior substitutions or ecological history alter the accessibility of a later adaptive phenotype.
* **H4 - reuse/co-option hypothesis:** complex trait evolution is better explained by modified deployment of pre-existing regulatory modules than by a wholly novel final-state program.

## Five-stage EVOTRACE ladder

| Stage | Question | Required output | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| E0 Scope | What lineage, trait, environment, and time window are being explained? | estimand and sampling passport | endpoint or ancestral comparator undefined |
| E1 Variation | Which heritable variants and phenotypes are reliably measured? | QC-ed genomic and phenotypic ledger | inadequate controls, relatedness, or measurement precision |
| E2 Transmission | Are trajectories consistent with selection, drift, mutation, or gene flow? | competing population-process models | no neutral/demographic alternative or no replication |
| E3 Mechanism | Does a candidate map through developmental or physiological intermediates to the trait? | perturbation/mediation evidence map | association only; no directionality or alternative explanation |
| E4 Evaluation | Does the account predict held-out populations, lineages, or interventions? | calibration, sensitivity, and replication decision | poor generalization or unreconciled model dependence |

## Innovation

EVOTRACE does not ask investigators to choose between population genetics and evolutionary developmental biology. It treats them as sequential links in a causal chain. The framework distinguishes a **selection signature**, a **trait association**, a **developmental mechanism**, and a **historical explanation**. These claims have different evidentiary standards, so a result can be informative at one level while remaining explicitly unresolved at another.

## Design-only boundary

This proposal contains no observed trajectories, selection coefficients, CRISPR outcomes, ancestral regulatory reconstructions, or organ-evolution results. Its planned analyses and thresholds must be preregistered before a future empirical implementation.
