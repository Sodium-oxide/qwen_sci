## Multi-route idea evolution

1. **Root question:** “Is the universe flat or closed?”
2. **Scientific reframing:** A global answer is not identifiable without a model; the operational task is to compare declared curvature models across independent observables.
3. **Mechanism route:** Explicitly map `Omega_K`, late-time expansion, recombination, CMB lensing amplitude, foreground model, and topology assumptions to each observable.
4. **Falsifiability route:** Require that a closed preference replicate in independent CMB spectra/lensing and remain compatible with BAO/SN geometry after every translation is declared.
5. **Adversarial route:** Challenge any apparent curvature signal with `A_L`, foreground, calibration, primordial-spectrum, dark-energy, and likelihood-correlation alternatives.
6. **Selection:** Preserve all channels as separate evidence cards and only issue a model-bounded result after compatibility and posterior-predictive tests.

## Selected primary direction

### D3 — CurvLedger: a cross-probe curvature-consistency framework

**Central claim.** A claimed preference for closed spatial curvature should be classified only after a versioned ledger links (i) the cosmological model and priors, (ii) dataset-specific likelihood and nuisance treatment, (iii) a forward observable map, (iv) shared-information accounting, and (v) tests against non-curvature explanations.

**Scientific object.** Curvature parameter `Omega_K` in FLRW model families, plus explicitly declared nuisance and extension parameters.

**Mechanism.** CMB acoustic peak locations and smoothing, CMB lensing reconstruction, BAO transverse/radial scales, and supernova relative distances respond differently to curvature and to alternatives. Agreement is therefore informative only when likelihoods, data overlap, sound-horizon calibration, and model translations are recorded.

**Primary hypotheses.**

- **H1 — Translation discipline:** preserving priors, curvature conventions, and forward-model assumptions prevents a conditional CMB limit from being misreported as a model-independent geometric measurement.
- **H2 — Replication discipline:** an apparent closed-curvature preference that is not reproduced in independent CMB/lensing analyses, or that fails joint posterior-predictive checks with BAO/SN, must be labeled channel-local rather than cross-validated.
- **H3 — Alternative discipline:** allowing an appropriate alternative set (`A_L`, foreground/calibration variants, primordial-spectrum flexibility, and expansion-history variants) changes the correct interpretation of a curvature preference more often than adding a second summary statistic.
- **H4 — Topology separation:** no CurvLedger status can become a topology claim without a topology-specific observable and likelihood.

**Falsification conditions.** H1 fails if a result is invariant after removing its stated prior/translation card. H2 fails if the framework classifies a single-channel result as cross-validated without independent-channel support. H3 fails if all declared alternatives produce the same predictive residual pattern as curvature. H4 fails if curvature information alone changes a topology status.

## Debate and selection audit

The debate compared D2 and D3 on the principal objection: “Does a bookkeeping framework merely redescribe the literature?” D3 wins because its outputs are testable: withheld likelihood cards, injected systematic shifts, and model-switch posterior-predictive tests can show whether its classifications are calibrated. D4 remains in the portfolio because topology is scientifically distinct, but it is not selected to answer the present Planck–multi-probe discrepancy.

## Portfolio status taxonomy

- `CURVATURE_CONSISTENT_WITH_ZERO`: included model/data combination provides no material evidence away from `Omega_K=0`.
- `CLOSED_PREFERENCE_CHANNEL_LOCAL`: a closed direction occurs only under a declared channel, likelihood, or model family.
- `CLOSED_PREFERENCE_CROSS_VALIDATED`: independent channels and alternatives support a common closed parameter region; this remains model-bounded.
- `MODEL_OR_LIKELIHOOD_INCONSISTENT`: no shared parameter region passes the compatibility test without an identified extension/systematic explanation.
- `TOPOLOGY_UNRESOLVED`: curvature evidence cannot establish global topology.
