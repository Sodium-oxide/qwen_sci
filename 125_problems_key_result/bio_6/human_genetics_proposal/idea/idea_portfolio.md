# Idea Agent: Candidate Portfolio and Direction Selection

## Candidate directions

### D1 -- ARHGAP11B replication and mechanistic refinement

- **Gap alignment:** G1, G3, G4.
- **Strength:** Direct, reciprocal organoid evidence makes `ARHGAP11B` an unusually strong anchor candidate.
- **Limitation:** A single-gene program cannot answer how multiple human-lineage genetic changes interact or why regulatory divergence is pervasive.
- **Portfolio decision:** Competitive control direction, not selected as the main proposal.

### D2 -- HAR-to-target-gene regulatory atlas

- **Gap alignment:** G1, G2, G4.
- **Strength:** Directly addresses noncoding human-lineage changes and three-dimensional chromatin rewiring.
- **Limitation:** A broad atlas risks producing correlations without a reciprocal cellular phenotype.
- **Portfolio decision:** Competitive direction; contributes the regulatory module to D3.

### D3 -- CortexSwitch: Reciprocal multiplex causal map of human-lineage cortical modules

- **Gap alignment:** G1, G2, G3, G4, G5.
- **Core idea:** Select a compact, evidence-qualified panel consisting of a duplicated-gene axis (`ARHGAP11B`/`NOTCH2NL`-related dosage state) and a small set of state-active HAR/3D-contact regulatory candidates. Introduce human-lineage states into chimpanzee lines and ancestral/orthologous states into human lines, then use time-resolved single-cell multiome and imaging endpoints to test whether the panel converges on a basal-radial-glia persistence to neurogenic-transition module.
- **Falsifiable prediction:** Reciprocal perturbations must produce sign-consistent, line-replicated changes in a prespecified progenitor-state contrast, with a corresponding molecular mediator. If the effect occurs in only one species direction, one line, one batch, or only after failure of a fidelity gate, D3 is not supported.
- **Novelty:** The primary unit is not a gene nor a differentially expressed transcript; it is a reciprocal, evidence-gated causal module whose coding and regulatory components can be compared alone and in combination.
- **Portfolio decision:** `SELECTED_PRIMARY_IDEA`.

### D4 -- Genome-wide comparative dependency screen

- **Gap alignment:** G1, G3, G4.
- **Strength:** Ambitious route for discovering unexpected species-specific genetic dependencies.
- **Limitation:** Too broad for a first causal evolutionary study and difficult to map to one interpretable cell-state chain.
- **Portfolio decision:** High-risk future expansion after D3 establishes benchmark quality and endpoints.

## Selected claim ceiling

`CortexSwitch` may support a conditional conclusion about a tested genetic module and a defined cortical progenitor transition. It may not claim to identify a complete set of genes that “make humans human,” explain cognition, or generalize from organoids to whole-organism evolution without additional evidence.
