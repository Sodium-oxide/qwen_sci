B. Nine Explicit Unresolved Items

Release as a research plan requires consistency of claims,
citations, labels, and expected-result language. Release as a
theorem paper requires substantially more: D1–D4 finalized;
PO1–PO4 closed; a valid comparator; an in-domain counterexample search; independent line-by-line proof review; and
explicit global-causal scope. Release of an astrophysical massgap claim additionally requires a selection-aware population
model and comparison against stellar alternatives.

The final author route retains the following nine unresolved
items.
1) exact AANEC definition, generator class, parameterization, and regularity;
2) exact independent averaged focusing condition and its
relation to AANEC;
3) quasi-local mass convention and compactness threshold;
4) precise trapped-surface predicate and strict/marginal distinction;
5) quantitative translation from dynamic compactness
to stable-remnant mass, including the collapse-toequilibrium matching rule;
6) classical versus semiclassical or quantum stress-energy
boundary;
7) proof that the averaged formulation is genuinely weaker
than its pointwise comparator;
8) measurement, calibration, data-governance, and reproducibility protocol for any numerical extension;
9) independent human review by mathematical-relativity,
compact-star, and population specialists.
All nine are scientific dependencies. A compiled PDF does
not resolve them.
