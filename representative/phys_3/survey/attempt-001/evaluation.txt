=== What are the limits of heat transfer in matter? Materials science research may help address high-power-density heat-transfer challenges across length scales, from nanometre-scale electronic devices and circuits to industrial-scale processes such as power plants. The research focus is to identify fundamental and material-dependent limits on thermal transport, interfacial thermal resistance, phonon and electron heat conduction, and heat dissipation under high power density.
